# Research archive

This directory contains the research and audit trail that led to the current
MemoryLineage scope. It is useful for provenance and submission preparation,
but it is not the product entry point.

- Candidate and score-loop documents record rejected alternatives and their
  evidence limits.
- Competitor audits record public submissions and their verified artifacts.
- Deep-research documents record standards, rules, and market comparisons.
- Executable abandoned experiments live under `research/spikes/`.

The current product-evolution decision is recorded in
[`2026-09-20-memorylineage-winning-patterns-and-product-evolution.md`](./2026-09-20-memorylineage-winning-patterns-and-product-evolution.md).
It preserves the MemoryLineage protocol and sharpens the product around
Restore Preflight, Recovery Rehearsal, and independently verifiable decision
receipts. It is a research decision, not evidence that all proposed work is
already implemented.

The expanded potential and product-maturity map is recorded in
[`2026-09-20-memorylineage-potential-expansion.md`](./2026-09-20-memorylineage-potential-expansion.md).
It covers runtime adapters, branch-aware recovery, provider migration,
post-load receipts, availability, privacy profiles, governance, conformance,
observability, and cost policy. These are staged opportunities, not current
product claims.

The follow-up release audit is recorded in
[`2026-09-20-memorylineage-strict-dominance-plan-audit.md`](./2026-09-20-memorylineage-strict-dominance-plan-audit.md).
It identifies source-level truthfulness issues, separates measurable technical
dominance from unavailable public-submission surfaces, and defines the P0–P3
gates required before making a competitive superiority claim.

The deeper novelty and impact review is recorded in
[`2026-09-20-memorylineage-uniqueness-impact-adoption-audit.md`](./2026-09-20-memorylineage-uniqueness-impact-adoption-audit.md).
It compares the product against current public memory, migration, checkpoint,
and continuity work, narrows the defensible Web3 wedge to recovery preflight
under independent authority, and defines evidence gates for impact without
confusing technical reproducibility with market adoption.

The current public competitor register is recorded in
[`2026-09-20-3rd-web-hack-public-project-audit.md`](./2026-09-20-3rd-web-hack-public-project-audit.md).
It records every public 3rd-Web-Hack candidate found in the current search,
the repository and demo links that were resolved, adjacent non-entry
benchmarks, projects with unverified event membership such as ExitDrill, and
the unpublished-gallery limitation. ExitDrill is separately discussed as a
bounded workflow reference in the
[design-quality contract](../product/design-quality.md#bounded-drill-with-visible-outcomes)
and the [uniqueness and adoption audit](./2026-09-20-memorylineage-uniqueness-impact-adoption-audit.md#exitdrill-adjacent-drill-and-explanation-pattern).

The peak-potential product and evidence plan is recorded in
[`2026-09-20-memorylineage-peak-potential-plan.md`](./2026-09-20-memorylineage-peak-potential-plan.md).
It separates verified observations from targets, incorporates lessons from the
public 3rd-Web-Hack field and Polkadot hackathon material, and defines the
recovery-gate, receipt, evidence, integration, and reproduction milestones
without claiming that proposed work is already complete.

The Polkadot portability and strategic ecosystem roadmap is recorded in
[`2026-09-20-memorylineage-polkadot-strategic-roadmap.md`](./2026-09-20-memorylineage-polkadot-strategic-roadmap.md).
It treats Polkadot Hub as a second execution anchor, separates REVM portability
from optional PVM research, defines the evidence and recovery-receipt model,
and lists the gates required before claiming cross-environment support.

The critical review of that roadmap is recorded in
[`2026-09-20-memorylineage-polkadot-roadmap-audit.md`](./2026-09-20-memorylineage-polkadot-roadmap-audit.md).
It identifies the cross-anchor identity, receipt authenticity, decision-vocabulary,
snapshot encoding, branch-semantics, availability, and runtime-enforcement gaps
that must be resolved before portability or cross-chain recovery claims are made.

Historical score files must not be presented as official judge scores. The
current evidence gate reports pass/fail conditions and deliberately does not
manufacture a Devpost score.

portable evidence, Inspector journey, and independent reproduction into one
