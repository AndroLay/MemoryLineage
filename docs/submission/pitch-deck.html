Change one commitment. Replay must fail.

Export the same evidence bundle from the Inspector and check it with the independent Rust CLI.

original → BUNDLE REPLAY VERIFIED
tampered → TRANSITION_ID_MISMATCH

Original contribution and limits

Recovery preflight around pinned registry semantics.

Built and evidenced here

  • Current, historical, divergent, and unverified classification.
  • Policy-bound recovery receipts and protected local loader.
  • Independent replay, authority timeline, and tamper evidence.
  • Rust/WASM Inspector and a reproducible local incident.

Outside the current proof

  • Semantic truth or safety of the private memory.
  • Production agent adoption or off-chain availability.
  • Public deployment of this exact Demo Space V2 history.
  • Canonical bundle provenance, complete history recovery, historical ERC-1271 replay, or RPC consensus.
  • Authorship of ERC-8350, EIP-712, or ERC-1271.
